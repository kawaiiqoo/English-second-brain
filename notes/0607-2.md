# clearly

## Meaning
Used to emphasize that something is obvious, definite, or easy to see/understand. It can be used seriously or with a slightly sarcastic tone.

## Example
She clearly didn't read the instructions.

## Related Expressions
- obviously
- plainly
- evidently

---

# genuinely

## Meaning
Sincerely, truly, or honestly; used to show true feelings or opinions. Stronger and more heartfelt than "really."

## Example
I genuinely have no idea what to do right now.

## Related Expressions
- sincerely
- honestly
- truly

---

# occasionally

## Meaning
Sometimes, but not often; used to describe something that happens from time to time in a more polished way than "sometimes."

## Example
I occasionally have a coffee in the afternoon but not every day.

## Related Expressions
- sometimes
- from time to time
- every so often

---

# randomly

## Meaning
Without pattern or order; unexpectedly or without planning. It's often used to talk about something surprising or sudden.

## Example
I randomly ran into my old teacher at the grocery store.

## Related Expressions
- unexpectedly
- out of the blue
- by chance

---

# typically

## Meaning
Usually, in most cases; describes a common or normal pattern, but allows for exceptions.

## Example
I typically wake up around 7 but today I slept in.

## Related Expressions
- usually
- normally
- generally

---

# conventionally

## Meaning
In a traditional or standard way; according to what is generally accepted or done.

## Example
Conventionally this role goes to someone with more experience.

## Related Expressions
- traditionally
- customarily
- usually
