# clearly

## Meaning
Used to emphasize that something is obvious, definite, or easy to see. It can also carry a slightly sarcastic tone.

## Example
- She clearly didn't read the instructions.
- He's clearly not over her. Look at the way he acts.

## Related Expressions
- obviously
- plainly
- evidently

---

# genuinely

## Meaning
Sincerely; truly. Stronger and more heartfelt than "really." Used to emphasize true feelings or opinions.

## Example
- I genuinely have no idea what to do right now.
- She genuinely cares about the people around her.

## Related Expressions
- sincerely
- honestly
- truly

---

# occasionally

## Meaning
Sometimes, but not often. More polished and formal than "sometimes." Describes things that happen from time to time.

## Example
- I occasionally have a coffee in the afternoon but not every day.
- We occasionally argue but we always work it out.

## Related Expressions
- sometimes
- from time to time
- every so often

---

# randomly

## Meaning
Suddenly or unexpectedly; without a specific pattern. Used for unpredictable events or when changing topics in conversation.

## Example
- I randomly ran into my old teacher at the grocery store.
- Random, but I've been thinking about you lately.

## Related Expressions
- out of the blue
- unexpectedly
- suddenly

---

# typically

## Meaning
Usually; in most cases. Describes usual habits but allows for exceptions.

## Example
- I typically wake up around 7 but today I slept in.
- She typically finishes work early on Fridays.

## Related Expressions
- usually
- generally
- normally

---

# conventionally

## Meaning
According to what is generally done or believed; following traditional rules or norms.

## Example
- Conventionally, this role goes to someone with more experience.
- She doesn't dress conventionally but it works for her.

## Related Expressions
- traditionally
- customarily
- normatively
