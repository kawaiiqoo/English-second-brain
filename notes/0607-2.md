# clearly

## Meaning
Used to emphasize a situation that is obvious or easy to see. It can also have a slightly sarcastic tone depending on the context.

## Example
She clearly didn't read the instructions.  
(걔 분명히 설명서를 안 읽었어.)

He's clearly not over her. Look at the way he acts.  
(걔 분명히 아직 못 잊은 거야. 행동을 봐봐.)

## Related Expressions
- obviously  
- plainly  
- evidently

# genuinely

## Meaning
Used to express sincere, real, or heartfelt emotions or opinions. Stronger and more heartfelt than "really".

## Example
I genuinely have no idea what to do right now.  
(진심으로 지금 뭘 해야 할지 모르겠어.)

She genuinely cares about the people around her.  
(걔는 주변 사람들을 진심으로 아껴.)

## Related Expressions
- honestly  
- truly  
- wholeheartedly  

---

# occasionally

## Meaning
Used to talk about something that happens sometimes, but not regularly or often. More sophisticated than "sometimes".

## Example
I occasionally have a coffee in the afternoon but not every day.  
(오후에 가끔 커피 마시는데 매일은 아니야.)

We occasionally argue but we always work it out.  
(우리가끔 싸우는데 항상 잘 해결해.)

## Related Expressions
- sometimes  
- from time to time  
- every now and then  

---

# randomly

## Meaning
Used to describe a sudden or unexpected event, thought, or action. Often used when something happens without planning.

## Example
I randomly ran into my old teacher at the grocery store.  
(마트에서 뜬금없이 옛날 선생님을 만났어.)

Random but I've been thinking about you lately.  
(뜬금없는데 요즘 네 생각이 자꾸 났어.)

## Related Expressions
- all of a sudden  
- out of the blue  
- unexpectedly  

---

# typically

## Meaning
Used to talk about what usually happens, showing that there can be exceptions.

## Example
I typically wake up around 7 but today I slept in.  
(보통은 7시쯤 일어나는데 오늘은 늦잠 잤어.)

She typically finishes work early on Fridays.  
(걔보통 금요일엔 일찍 끝내.)

## Related Expressions
- usually  
- generally  
- as a rule  

---

# conventionally

## Meaning
Describes what is accepted as standard or traditional, according to common practices or norms.

## Example
Conventionally this role goes to someone with more experience.  
(관례적으로 이 역할은 경험이 더 많은 사람한테 가.)

She doesn't dress conventionally but it works for her.  
(걔 통상적인 방식으로 옷을 입지 않는데 걔한테는 잘 어울려.)

## Related Expressions
- traditionally  
- customarily  
- according to convention
