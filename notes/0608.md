# Beat around the bush

## Meaning  
To avoid talking about what is important by speaking indirectly or in a roundabout way. (빙빙 돌려 말하다)

## Example  
Stop beating around the bush and tell me what you really think.

## Related Expressions  
- Go around in circles  
- Dodge the question  
- Talk in circles  

---

# Get the hang of it

## Meaning  
To learn how to do something, especially if it's not easy at first; to get the sense or skill for something. (감을 잡다)

## Example  
Don’t worry, you’ll get the hang of it after a few tries.

## Related Expressions  
- Get used to  
- Figure out  
- Master  

---

# Bite the bullet

## Meaning  
To force yourself to do something unpleasant or difficult, or to be brave in a difficult situation. (꾹 참고 하다)

## Example  
I know the dentist scares you, but you’ll just have to bite the bullet.

## Related Expressions  
- Suck it up  
- Face the music  
- Grin and bear it  

---

# Clear the air

## Meaning  
To discuss misunderstandings or disagreements to remove tension or hard feelings. (오해를 풀다)

## Example  
We had an argument, but we sat down and cleared the air.

## Related Expressions  
- Make amends  
- Squash the beef  
- Settle things  

---

# Jump to conclusions

## Meaning  
To decide something without having all the facts; to make a hasty judgment. (성급하게 결론 내리다)

## Example  
Don’t jump to conclusions before hearing his side of the story.

## Related Expressions  
- Assume  
- Rush to judgment  
- Make assumptions
