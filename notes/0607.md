# clearly

## Meaning
Used to emphasize that something is obvious or easy to see or understand. It can also be used sarcastically to point out someone's mistake or misunderstanding.

## Example
- She clearly didn't read the instructions.
- He's clearly not over her. Look at the way he acts.

## Related Expressions
- obviously
- plainly
- undoubtedly

---

# genuinely

## Meaning
Sincerely; truly. Used to show that you really mean what you say, or to add emotional weight to your statement. Stronger and more heartfelt than "really" or "truly."

## Example
- I genuinely have no idea what to do right now.
- She genuinely cares about the people around her.

## Related Expressions
- sincerely
- honestly
- truly

---

# occasionally

## Meaning
Sometimes; from time to time; not regularly or often. A more sophisticated way to say "sometimes."

## Example
- I occasionally have a coffee in the afternoon but not every day.
- We occasionally argue but we always work it out.

## Related Expressions
- sometimes
- from time to time
- every now and then

---

# randomly

## Meaning
Suddenly or without a plan or reason; unexpectedly. Used when something happens by chance or appears out of nowhere.

## Example
- I randomly ran into my old teacher at the grocery store.
- Random but I've been thinking about you lately.

## Related Expressions
- out of the blue
- by chance
- unexpectedly

---

# typically

## Meaning
Usually; in most cases; normally. Used to describe a common pattern or habit, but allows for exceptions.

## Example
- I typically wake up around 7 but today I slept in.
- She typically finishes work early on Fridays.

## Related Expressions
- usually
- normally
- generally

---

# conventionally

## Meaning
According to traditional or generally accepted rules or customs; in a standard or typical way.

## Example
- Conventionally this role goes to someone with more experience.
- She doesn't dress conventionally but it works for her.

## Related Expressions
- traditionally
- customarily
- ordinarily
