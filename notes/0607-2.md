# clearly

## Meaning
Used to show that something is obvious, without a doubt, or very easy to see or understand. It can also be used sarcastically.

## Example
She clearly didn't read the instructions.  
(걔 분명히 설명서를 안 읽었어.)

## Related Expressions
obviously, evidently, plainly

---

# genuinely

## Meaning
Truly or sincerely; used to emphasize real feelings, opinions, or situations. Stronger and more heartfelt than “really.”

## Example
I genuinely have no idea what to do right now.  
(진심으로 지금 뭘 해야 할지 모르겠어.)

## Related Expressions
sincerely, truly, honestly

---

# occasionally

## Meaning
Sometimes, but not often; more sophisticated and less common than “sometimes.”

## Example
I occasionally have a coffee in the afternoon, but not every day.  
(오후에 가끔 커피 마시는데 매일은 아니야.)

## Related Expressions
sometimes, from time to time, every so often

---

# randomly

## Meaning
Without a specific plan or pattern; used when something happens by chance or unexpectedly. Often used to change the topic suddenly in conversation.

## Example
I randomly ran into my old teacher at the grocery store.  
(마트에서 뜬금없이 옛날 선생님을 만났어.)

## Related Expressions
by chance, out of the blue, unexpectedly

---

# typically

## Meaning
Usually; most of the time, but there may be exceptions. Used to talk about general patterns or habits.

## Example
I typically wake up around 7, but today I slept in.  
(보통은 7시쯤 일어나는데 오늘은 늦잠 잤어.)

## Related Expressions
usually, generally, normally

---

# conventionally

## Meaning
In a way that follows what is generally done or accepted; traditionally or according to standard practice.

## Example
Conventionally, this role goes to someone with more experience.  
(관례적으로 이 역할은 경험이 더 많은 사람한테 가.)

## Related Expressions
traditionally, customarily, according to convention
