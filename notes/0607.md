# clearly

## Meaning
Obviously; without a doubt; used to emphasize that a situation or fact is obvious to everyone. Can also be used sarcastically.

## Example
- She clearly didn't read the instructions.
- He's clearly not over her. Look at the way he acts.

## Related Expressions
- obviously
- evidently
- plainly

---

# genuinely

## Meaning
Truly; sincerely; with real, heartfelt emotion or intent. Stronger and more sincere than "really."

## Example
- I genuinely have no idea what to do right now.
- She genuinely cares about the people around her.

## Related Expressions
- truly
- sincerely
- honestly

---

# occasionally

## Meaning
Sometimes; from time to time; not often but happening now and then. More sophisticated than "sometimes."

## Example
- I occasionally have a coffee in the afternoon but not every day.
- We occasionally argue but we always work it out.

## Related Expressions
- sometimes
- from time to time
- every so often

---

# randomly

## Meaning
Suddenly or without warning; unexpectedly; describes actions or thoughts that happen without planning, often surprising or not related to the situation.

## Example
- I randomly ran into my old teacher at the grocery store.
- Random, but I've been thinking about you lately.

## Related Expressions
- out of the blue
- unexpectedly
- all of a sudden

---

# typically

## Meaning
Usually; in most cases; describes what normally happens, but allows for exceptions.

## Example
- I typically wake up around 7 but today I slept in.
- She typically finishes work early on Fridays.

## Related Expressions
- usually
- generally
- ordinarily

---

# conventionally

## Meaning
According to what is generally done or accepted; following traditional ways or standards.

## Example
- Conventionally, this role goes to someone with more experience.
- She doesn't dress conventionally but it works for her.

## Related Expressions
- traditionally
- customarily
- in the usual way
