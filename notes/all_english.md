# clearly

## Meaning

"Clearly" is an adverb used to show that something is obvious, easy to understand, or without any doubt.

## Example

- She spoke clearly so everyone could understand her.
- Clearly, he didn’t want to attend the meeting.

## Related Expressions

- obviously
- plainly
- evidently
- without a doubt
