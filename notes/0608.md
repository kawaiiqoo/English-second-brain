# Beat around the bush

## Meaning
To avoid talking directly about the main topic; to speak in a roundabout or indirect way.

## Example
Stop beating around the bush and tell me what you really think.

## Related Expressions
- Get to the point
- Come straight to the point
- Talk in circles

# Get the hang of it

## Related Expressions

# Bite the bullet

## Meaning
To do something difficult or unpleasant that you have been avoiding.

## Example
I don't want to go to the dentist, but I'll have to bite the bullet and make an appointment.

## Related Expressions
- Face the music
- Suck it up
- Take it on the chin

# Jump to conclusions

## Meaning
To make a decision or judgment too quickly, without enough information.

## Example
Don't jump to conclusions until you know what really happened.

## Related Expressions
- Rush to judgment
- Presume
- Prejudge
