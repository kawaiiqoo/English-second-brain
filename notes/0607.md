# clearly

## Meaning
Used to emphasize a situation is obvious, plain, or undeniable. Can also be used in a sarcastic or ironic way.

## Example
She clearly didn't read the instructions.  
(걔 분명히 설명서를 안 읽었어.)

He's clearly not over her. Look at the way he acts.  
(걔 분명히 아직 못 잊은 거야. 행동을 봐봐.)

## Related Expressions
- obviously
- plainly
- evidently

# genuinely

## Meaning
Used to express sincerity or true feelings. Much stronger than "really," showing something is heartfelt or authentic.

## Example
I genuinely have no idea what to do right now.  
(진심으로 지금 뭘 해야 할지 모르겠어.)

She genuinely cares about the people around her.  
(걔는 주변 사람들을 진심으로 아껴.)

## Related Expressions
- sincerely
- honestly
- truly

# occasionally

## Meaning
Means sometimes, but suggests something happens from time to time, not often. A more sophisticated alternative to "sometimes."

## Example
I occasionally have a coffee in the afternoon but not every day.  
(오후에 가끔 커피 마시는데 매일은 아니야.)

We occasionally argue but we always work it out.  
(우리가 가끔 싸우는데 항상 잘 해결해.)

## Related Expressions
- sometimes
- from time to time
- once in a while

# randomly

## Meaning
Used when something happens without planning or expectation, suddenly, or without pattern. Also used to introduce an unrelated topic.

## Example
I randomly ran into my old teacher at the grocery store.  
(마트에서 뜬금없이 옛날 선생님을 만났어.)

Random, but I've been thinking about you lately.  
(뜬금없는데 요즘 네 생각이 자꾸 났어.)

## Related Expressions
- out of the blue
- unexpectedly
- all of a sudden

---

# typically

## Meaning
Means "usually" or "generally." Used to express a usual habit, pattern, or trend, but allows for exceptions.

## Example
I typically wake up around 7 but today I slept in.  
(보통은 7시쯤 일어나는데 오늘은 늦잠 잤어.)

She typically finishes work early on Fridays.  
(걔 보통 금요일엔 일찍 끝내.)

## Related Expressions
- usually
- generally
- normally

---

# conventionally

## Meaning
Means "according to what is generally accepted or done." Refers to traditional or normal ways, standards, or customs.

## Example
Conventionally, this role goes to someone with more experience.  
(관례적으로 이 역할은 경험이 더 많은 사람한테 가.)

She doesn't dress conventionally but it works for her.  
(걔 통상적인 방식으로 옷을 입지 않는데 걔한테는 잘 어울려.)

## Related Expressions
- traditionally
- customarily
- ordinarily
