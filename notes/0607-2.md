# clearly

## Meaning
Used to emphasize that something is obvious, easy to see, or undeniably true. Often highlights situations that anyone can notice. It can also be used sarcastically to point out someone's mistake or oversight.

## Example
- She clearly didn't read the instructions.
- He's clearly not over her. Look at the way he acts.

## Related Expressions
- obviously
- evidently
- plainly

---

# genuinely

## Meaning
Used to express sincerity or true feelings/emotions. It is stronger than "really" and carries a sense of honesty and depth.

## Example
- I genuinely have no idea what to do right now.
- She genuinely cares about the people around her.

## Related Expressions
- honestly
- truly
- sincerely

---

# occasionally

## Meaning
Means "sometimes," but is considered more refined. Used to talk about things that happen from time to time, but not regularly.

## Example
- I occasionally have a coffee in the afternoon but not every day.
- We occasionally argue but we always work it out.

## Related Expressions
- sometimes
- from time to time
- every so often

---

# randomly

## Meaning
Describes something happening without pattern or reason, or suddenly/unexpectedly. Often used in casual conversation to describe unexpected occurrences or to smoothly change the topic.

## Example
- I randomly ran into my old teacher at the grocery store.
- Random but I've been thinking about you lately.

## Related Expressions
- suddenly
- out of the blue
- unexpectedly

---

# typically

## Meaning
Used to describe usual, habitual, or standard patterns or behaviors, while leaving room for exceptions.

## Example
- I typically wake up around 7 but today I slept in.
- She typically finishes work early on Fridays.

## Related Expressions
- usually
- normally
- generally

---

# conventionally

## Meaning
Describes something done in a traditional or widely accepted way; according to convention or established custom.

## Example
- Conventionally this role goes to someone with more experience.
- She doesn't dress conventionally but it works for her.

## Related Expressions
- traditionally
- customarily
- ordinarily
