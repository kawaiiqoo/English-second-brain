# Clearly

## Meaning
Obviously; without any doubt; used to emphasize a situation that anyone can recognize. Can sometimes be used sarcastically.

## Example
She clearly didn't read the instructions.  
He's clearly not over her. Look at the way he acts.

## Related Expressions
- Obviously
- Plainly
- Evidently

---

# Genuinely

## Meaning
Truly; sincerely; with real feeling or honesty. Stronger and more heartfelt than "really."

## Example
I genuinely have no idea what to do right now.  
She genuinely cares about the people around her.

## Related Expressions
- Sincerely
- Honestly
- Truly

---

# Occasionally

## Meaning
Sometimes, but not often; more stylish and less common than "sometimes." Used to talk about things that happen from time to time.

## Example
I occasionally have a coffee in the afternoon but not every day.  
We occasionally argue but we always work it out.

## Related Expressions
- Sometimes
- Every so often
- From time to time

---

# Randomly

## Meaning
Suddenly and without any obvious reason or pattern; unexpectedly. Also used when something comes up out of nowhere in conversation.

## Example
I randomly ran into my old teacher at the grocery store.  
Random, but I've been thinking about you lately.

## Related Expressions
- Out of the blue
- Unexpectedly
- Spontaneously

---

# Typically

## Meaning
Usually; in most cases; what generally happens, though exceptions are possible.

## Example
I typically wake up around 7 but today I slept in.  
She typically finishes work early on Fridays.

## Related Expressions
- Usually
- Normally
- Generally

---

# Conventionally

## Meaning
According to accepted customs, traditions, or standards; as is usually done by most people.

## Example
Conventionally, this role goes to someone with more experience.  
She doesn't dress conventionally but it works for her.

## Related Expressions
- Traditionally
- Customarily
- Normally
