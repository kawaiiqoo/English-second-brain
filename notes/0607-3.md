# I can’t get enough of~

## Meaning

This expression means that you like something so much that you always want more of it. You never feel bored or tired of it.

## Example

I can’t get enough of chocolate. I eat it every day!

## Related Expressions

- I’m addicted to ~
- I’m crazy about ~
- I just love ~
