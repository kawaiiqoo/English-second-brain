# clearly

## Meaning

"Clearly" is an adverb that means "in a way that is easy to see, hear, or understand." It is often used to show that something is obvious or without doubt.

## Example

She spoke very clearly, so everyone understood her instructions.

## Related Expressions

- obviously
- plainly
- evidently
- unmistakably
