# Clearly

## Meaning
- Obviously; without any doubt.  
- Used to emphasize that something is easy to see, understand, or notice.  
- Can be used both seriously or with a hint of sarcasm.

## Example
- She clearly didn't read the instructions.  
  (걔 분명히 설명서를 안 읽었어.)
- He's clearly not over her. Look at the way he acts.  
  (걔 분명히 아직 못 잊은 거야. 행동을 봐봐.)

## Related Expressions
- Obviously  
- Evidently  
- Plainly

# Genuinely

## Meaning
- Truly; sincerely; honestly.  
- Stronger and more heartfelt than "really."  
- Adds seriousness to feelings or opinions.

## Example
- I genuinely have no idea what to do right now.  
  (진심으로 지금 뭘 해야 할지 모르겠어.)
- She genuinely cares about the people around her.  
  (걔는 주변 사람들을 진심으로 아껴.)

## Related Expressions
- Honestly  
- Sincerely  
- Truly

# Occasionally

## Meaning
- Sometimes; from time to time; not often but at intervals.  
- More sophisticated and less casual than "sometimes."

## Example
- I occasionally have a coffee in the afternoon but not every day.  
  (오후에 가끔 커피 마시는데 매일은 아니야.)
- We occasionally argue but we always work it out.  
  (우리 가끔 싸우는데 항상 잘 해결해.)

## Related Expressions
- Sometimes  
- From time to time  
- Every so often

# Randomly

## Meaning
- Suddenly; unexpectedly; without any pattern or plan.  
- Used when something happens without a clear reason.  
- Often used to introduce a new topic in conversation, like "by the way."

## Example
- I randomly ran into my old teacher at the grocery store.  
  (마트에서 뜬금없이 옛날 선생님을 만났어.)
- Random, but I've been thinking about you lately.  
  (뜬금없는데 요즘 네 생각이 자꾸 났어.)

## Related Expressions
- Out of the blue  
- Unexpectedly  
- By chance

# Typically

## Meaning
- Usually; most of the time; in most cases.  
- Shows something is a common pattern, but not always true.

## Example
- I typically wake up around 7 but today I slept in.  
  (보통은 7시쯤 일어나는데 오늘은 늦잠 잤어.)
- She typically finishes work early on Fridays.  
  (걔 보통 금요일엔 일찍 끝내.)

## Related Expressions
- Usually  
- Normally  
- Generally  

---

# Conventionally

## Meaning
- According to accepted standards or traditions; in the usual way.  
- Used for things done in a traditional or widely accepted manner.

## Example
- Conventionally, this role goes to someone with more experience.  
  (관례적으로 이 역할은 경험이 더 많은 사람한테 가.)
- She doesn't dress conventionally but it works for her.  
  (걔 통상적인 방식으로 옷을 입지 않는데 걔한테는 잘 어울려.)

## Related Expressions
- Traditionally  
- Customarily  
- As a rule
