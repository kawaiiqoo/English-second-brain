# clearly

## Meaning
- Certainly; without any doubt.  
- Used to emphasize that a situation or fact is obvious to everyone.
- Can also have a sarcastic nuance.

## Example
- She clearly didn't read the instructions.  
- He's clearly not over her. Look at the way he acts.

## Related Expressions
- obviously
- plainly
- evidently
- unmistakably

---

# genuinely

## Meaning
- Truly; sincerely; honestly.
- Used to emphasize real feelings or opinions.

## Example
- I genuinely have no idea what to do right now.  
- She genuinely cares about the people around her.

## Related Expressions
- sincerely
- honestly
- truly
- wholeheartedly

---

# occasionally

## Meaning
- Sometimes; now and then; from time to time.
- Used for things that happen, but not frequently.

## Example
- I occasionally have a coffee in the afternoon but not every day.  
- We occasionally argue but we always work it out.

## Related Expressions
- sometimes
- from time to time
- every so often
- once in a while

---

# randomly

## Meaning
- Without a specific pattern or purpose; suddenly or unexpectedly.
- Used when something happens by chance or unexpectedly, or to shift topics in conversation.

## Example
- I randomly ran into my old teacher at the grocery store.  
- Random, but I've been thinking about you lately.

## Related Expressions
- suddenly
- out of the blue
- by chance
- unexpectedly

---

# typically

## Meaning
- Usually; in most cases; as is usual.
- Used to describe a usual pattern, leaving room for exceptions.

## Example
- I typically wake up around 7 but today I slept in.  
- She typically finishes work early on Fridays.

## Related Expressions
- usually
- normally
- generally
- ordinarily

---

# conventionally

## Meaning
- In a way that is traditional or generally accepted.
- Used for what is standard or normal, especially in habits, practices, or appearance.

## Example
- Conventionally, this role goes to someone with more experience.  
- She doesn't dress conventionally but it works for her.

## Related Expressions
- traditionally
- customarily
- ordinarily
- typically
