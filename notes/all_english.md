# clearly

## Meaning

"Clearly" is an adverb that means something is easy to see, hear, or understand. It shows that something is obvious or expressed in a direct way.

## Example

She spoke clearly so that everyone could understand her.

## Related Expressions

- obviously
- plainly
- evidently
- distinctly
