# clearly

## Meaning

The adverb "clearly" means "in a way that is easy to see, hear, or understand." It can also mean "obviously" or "without doubt."

## Example

- She spoke clearly so that everyone could understand her.
- It was clearly a mistake; nobody meant to break the vase.

## Related Expressions

- obviously
- plainly
- distinctly
- without a doubt
