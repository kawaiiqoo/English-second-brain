# clearly

## Meaning
Used to emphasize that something is obvious or easy to understand; sometimes used with a sarcastic or ironic nuance.

## Example
- She clearly didn't read the instructions.
- He's clearly not over her. Look at the way he acts.

## Related Expressions
- obviously
- plainly
- evidently

---

# genuinely

## Meaning
Sincerely or truly; shows a stronger sense of realness or honesty than "really," especially when describing feelings or opinions.

## Example
- I genuinely have no idea what to do right now.
- She genuinely cares about the people around her.

## Related Expressions
- sincerely
- honestly
- truly

---

# occasionally

## Meaning
Sometimes, but not often; more refined than "sometimes," used for things that happen infrequently.

## Example
- I occasionally have a coffee in the afternoon but not every day.
- We occasionally argue but we always work it out.

## Related Expressions
- sometimes
- from time to time
- every now and then

---

# randomly

## Meaning
Suddenly or without warning; used when something unexpected happens or to change the topic naturally in conversation.

## Example
- I randomly ran into my old teacher at the grocery store.
- Random, but I've been thinking about you lately.

## Related Expressions
- out of the blue
- unexpectedly
- all of a sudden

---

# typically

## Meaning
Usually; to express general patterns or habits, but allows for exceptions.

## Example
- I typically wake up around 7 but today I slept in.
- She typically finishes work early on Fridays.

## Related Expressions
- usually
- generally
- normally

---

# conventionally

## Meaning
According to what is generally done, accepted, or traditional; describes standards or customs.

## Example
- Conventionally, this role goes to someone with more experience.
- She doesn't dress conventionally but it works for her.

## Related Expressions
- traditionally
- customarily
- ordinarily
