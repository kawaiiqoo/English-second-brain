# Clearly

## Meaning
Used to emphasize that something is obvious, easy to see, or without doubt. Can be used for both sincere and sarcastic situations.

## Example
- She clearly didn't read the instructions.
- He's clearly not over her. Look at the way he acts.

## Related Expressions
- obviously
- evidently
- plainly

---

# Genuinely

## Meaning
Means "truly" or "sincerely"; shows that a feeling, opinion, or action is real, not fake or forced.

## Example
- I genuinely have no idea what to do right now.
- She genuinely cares about the people around her.

## Related Expressions
- truly
- sincerely
- honestly

---

# Occasionally

## Meaning
Means "sometimes," but suggests it happens not frequently; a more sophisticated way of saying "sometimes."

## Example
- I occasionally have a coffee in the afternoon but not every day.
- We occasionally argue but we always work it out.

## Related Expressions
- sometimes
- from time to time
- every so often

---

# Randomly

## Meaning
Means "suddenly" or "without any clear reason or plan." Often used when something happens by surprise or unexpectedly.

## Example
- I randomly ran into my old teacher at the grocery store.
- Random but I've been thinking about you lately.

## Related Expressions
- out of the blue
- suddenly
- unexpectedly

---

# Typically

## Meaning
Means "usually" or "generally"; describes an action or situation that is common or expected, but allows for exceptions.

## Example
- I typically wake up around 7 but today I slept in.
- She typically finishes work early on Fridays.

## Related Expressions
- usually
- generally
- normally

---

# Conventionally

## Meaning
Means "according to tradition" or "the usual way"; used to describe actions or things that follow accepted standards or customs.

## Example
- Conventionally, this role goes to someone with more experience.
- She doesn't dress conventionally but it works for her.

## Related Expressions
- traditionally
- customarily
- ordinarily
