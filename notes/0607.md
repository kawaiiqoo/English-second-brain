# clearly

## Meaning
Used to emphasize that something is obvious or easily understood. It can also have a slightly sarcastic tone depending on context.

## Example
- She clearly didn't read the instructions.
- He's clearly not over her. Look at the way he acts.

## Related Expressions
- obviously
- plainly
- evidently

---

# genuinely

## Meaning
Truly or sincerely. Used to stress that something is heartfelt or real, stronger than just "really."

## Example
- I genuinely have no idea what to do right now.
- She genuinely cares about the people around her.

## Related Expressions
- sincerely
- honestly
- truly

---

# occasionally

## Meaning
Happening sometimes, but not regularly or frequently. More polished than "sometimes."

## Example
- I occasionally have a coffee in the afternoon but not every day.
- We occasionally argue but we always work it out.

## Related Expressions
- sometimes
- from time to time
- once in a while

---

# randomly

## Meaning
Without a specific pattern, reason, or plan; unexpectedly. Often used to talk about sudden, surprising situations.

## Example
- I randomly ran into my old teacher at the grocery store.
- Random but I've been thinking about you lately.

## Related Expressions
- out of the blue
- by chance
- unexpectedly

---

# typically

## Meaning
Usually; in most cases; describes a usual pattern or habit, but allows for exceptions.

## Example
- I typically wake up around 7 but today I slept in.
- She typically finishes work early on Fridays.

## Related Expressions
- usually
- generally
- normally

---

# conventionally

## Meaning
According to accepted customs, traditions, or norms; in the usual or traditional way.

## Example
- Conventionally, this role goes to someone with more experience.
- She doesn't dress conventionally but it works for her.

## Related Expressions
- traditionally
- customarily
- normally
