# clearly

## Meaning  
Used to emphasize that something is obvious or easy to understand. Can be used to state facts or, sometimes, with a slightly sarcastic tone.

## Example  
- She clearly didn't read the instructions.  
- He's clearly not over her. Look at the way he acts.

## Related Expressions  
- obviously  
- plainly  
- evidently  


# genuinely

## Meaning  
Sincerely; really and truly. Shows honest emotion or opinion, stronger and more heartfelt than "really."

## Example  
- I genuinely have no idea what to do right now.  
- She genuinely cares about the people around her.

## Related Expressions  
- honestly  
- sincerely  
- truly  


# occasionally

## Meaning  
Sometimes, but not very often; used to talk about something that happens from time to time.

## Example  
- I occasionally have a coffee in the afternoon but not every day.  
- We occasionally argue but we always work it out.

## Related Expressions  
- sometimes  
- from time to time  
- every now and then  


# randomly

## Meaning  
Without a specific plan or pattern; suddenly or unexpectedly. Also used to start a new, unrelated topic in conversation.

## Example  
- I randomly ran into my old teacher at the grocery store.  
- Random, but I've been thinking about you lately.

## Related Expressions  
- out of the blue  
- by chance  
- unexpectedly  


# typically

## Meaning  
Usually; in most cases. Used to describe what generally happens, while allowing for exceptions.

## Example  
- I typically wake up around 7 but today I slept in.  
- She typically finishes work early on Fridays.

## Related Expressions  
- usually  
- generally  
- normally  


# conventionally

## Meaning  
In a way that is traditional or generally accepted by most people; according to custom.

## Example  
- Conventionally, this role goes to someone with more experience.  
- She doesn't dress conventionally but it works for her.

## Related Expressions  
- traditionally  
- customarily  
- ordinarily
