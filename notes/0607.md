# Clearly

## Meaning
Used to emphasize that something is obvious or easy to see/understand. It can also have a slightly sarcastic tone.

## Example
- She clearly didn't read the instructions.  
- He's clearly not over her. Look at the way he acts.

## Related Expressions
- Obviously
- Plainly
- Evidently

---

# Genuinely

## Meaning
Sincerely or truly; used to show real feelings or opinions. More heartfelt than "really".

## Example
- I genuinely have no idea what to do right now.
- She genuinely cares about the people around her.

## Related Expressions
- Sincerely
- Honestly
- Truly

---

# Occasionally

## Meaning
Sometimes; not often but happening from time to time. More refined than "sometimes".

## Example
- I occasionally have a coffee in the afternoon but not every day.
- We occasionally argue but we always work it out.

## Related Expressions
- Sometimes
- From time to time
- Once in a while

---

# Randomly

## Meaning
Suddenly or without planning; used when something unexpected happens, or to abruptly change the topic in conversation.

## Example
- I randomly ran into my old teacher at the grocery store.
- Random, but I've been thinking about you lately.

## Related Expressions
- Out of the blue
- Unexpectedly
- Suddenly

---

# Typically

## Meaning
Usually, in most cases; describes regular patterns or habits, leaving room for exceptions.

## Example
- I typically wake up around 7 but today I slept in.
- She typically finishes work early on Fridays.

## Related Expressions
- Usually
- Generally
- Normally

---

# Conventionally

## Meaning
According to what is generally done or accepted; describes something traditional or standard.

## Example
- Conventionally, this role goes to someone with more experience.
- She doesn't dress conventionally but it works for her.

## Related Expressions
- Traditionally
- Customarily
- Normally
