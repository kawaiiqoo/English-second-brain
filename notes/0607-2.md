# Clearly

## Meaning
Used to emphasize that something is obvious or easy to see/understand; often used to highlight a situation or fact that is apparent to everyone. Can have a sarcastic or ironic tone.

## Example
- She clearly didn't read the instructions.  
  (걔 분명히 설명서를 안 읽었어.)
- He's clearly not over her. Look at the way he acts.  
  (걔 분명히 아직 못 잊은 거야. 행동을 봐봐.)

## Related Expressions
- obviously
- apparently
- evidently

# Genuinely

## Meaning
Sincerely; truly; with real feeling or authenticity. Stronger and more heartfelt than "really." Used to emphasize true feelings or opinions.

## Example
- I genuinely have no idea what to do right now.  
  (진심으로 지금 뭘 해야 할지 모르겠어.)
- She genuinely cares about the people around her.  
  (걔는 주변 사람들을 진심으로 아껴.)

## Related Expressions
- sincerely
- truly
- honestly

# Occasionally

## Meaning
Sometimes, but not regularly or often. A more sophisticated way of saying "sometimes." Used to describe things that happen now and then.

## Example
- I occasionally have a coffee in the afternoon, but not every day.  
  (오후에 가끔 커피 마시는데 매일은 아니야.)
- We occasionally argue but we always work it out.  
  (우리가끔 싸우는데 항상 잘 해결해.)

## Related Expressions
- sometimes
- every now and then
- from time to time

# Randomly

## Meaning
Unexpectedly or without a specific pattern; out of the blue. Used when something happens without planning or logical reason, or to change the topic suddenly.

## Example
- I randomly ran into my old teacher at the grocery store.  
  (마트에서 뜬금없이 옛날 선생님을 만났어.)
- Random but I've been thinking about you lately.  
  (뜬금없는데 요즘 네 생각이 자꾸 났어.)

## Related Expressions
- out of the blue
- unexpectedly
- suddenly

# Typically

## Meaning
Usually or normally; describes a usual pattern or habit, while allowing for exceptions.

## Example
- I typically wake up around 7 but today I slept in.  
  (보통은 7시쯤 일어나는데 오늘은 늦잠 잤어.)
- She typically finishes work early on Fridays.  
  (걔 보통 금요일엔 일찍 끝내.)

## Related Expressions
- usually
- generally
- normally

---

# Conventionally

## Meaning
In a way that is traditional or generally accepted; according to common standards or customs.

## Example
- Conventionally, this role goes to someone with more experience.  
  (관례적으로 이 역할은 경험이 더 많은 사람한테 가.)
- She doesn't dress conventionally but it works for her.  
  (걔 통상적인 방식으로 옷을 입지 않는데 걔한테는 잘 어울려.)

## Related Expressions
- traditionally
- customarily
- typically
