# clearly

## Meaning
- Obviously; without any doubt.  
- Used to emphasize that something is easy to see, understand, or know.
- Can sometimes have a sarcastic or ironic tone.

## Example
- She clearly didn’t read the instructions.  
- He’s clearly not over her. Look at the way he acts.

## Related Expressions
- obviously
- plainly
- evidently

---

# genuinely

## Meaning
- Truly; sincerely; honestly.  
- Expresses real and deep feelings or opinions (stronger than "really").

## Example
- I genuinely have no idea what to do right now.
- She genuinely cares about the people around her.

## Related Expressions
- truly
- honestly
- sincerely
- really (less strong)

---

# occasionally

## Meaning
- Sometimes, but not often; from time to time.  
- More sophisticated than “sometimes.” 

## Example
- I occasionally have a coffee in the afternoon but not every day.
- We occasionally argue but we always work it out.

## Related Expressions
- sometimes
- from time to time
- every so often

---

# randomly

## Meaning
- Without a specific pattern, purpose, or plan; unexpectedly.  
- Used when something happens suddenly or out of nowhere.

## Example
- I randomly ran into my old teacher at the grocery store.
- Random, but I’ve been thinking about you lately.

## Related Expressions
- suddenly
- out of the blue
- unexpectedly

---

# typically

## Meaning
- Usually; in most cases; as a rule.  
- Describes common patterns or habits, but allows for exceptions.

## Example
- I typically wake up around 7 but today I slept in.
- She typically finishes work early on Fridays.

## Related Expressions
- usually
- generally
- normally

---

# conventionally

## Meaning
- In a traditional or usual way; according to accepted standards.  
- Describes what is typical or expected in society or a group.

## Example
- Conventionally, this role goes to someone with more experience.
- She doesn’t dress conventionally but it works for her.

## Related Expressions
- traditionally
- normally
- customarily
