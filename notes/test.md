# I can't help ~ing

## Meaning

This expression is used when you cannot stop yourself from feeling, thinking, or doing something, even if you want to. It means that it's impossible not to do it.

## Example

- I can’t help worrying about my future.  
  (내 미래가 걱정될 수밖에 없어.)
- I can’t help thinking that we made the wrong decision.  
  (우리가 잘못된 결정을 내렸다는 생각이 자꾸 들어.)
