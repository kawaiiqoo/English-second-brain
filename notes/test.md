# I bet.

## Meaning

"I bet" is an informal expression used to show that you strongly believe something is true or that you understand what someone is feeling. It can also be used to agree with someone or to express empathy, excitement, or surprise about a situation.

## Example

A: "I had to work late every night this week."  
B: "I bet you're really tired!"

## Related Expressions

- "I can imagine."
- "I guess so."
- "No kidding."
- "For sure."
