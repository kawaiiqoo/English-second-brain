# clearly

## Meaning

"Clearly" is an adverb used to show that something is obvious, easy to understand, or without any doubt.

## Example

- She was clearly upset when she heard the news.
- Clearly, we need to try a different solution.

## Related Expressions

- obviously
- plainly
- evidently
- without a doubt
