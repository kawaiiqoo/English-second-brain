# clearly

## Meaning
Very obviously; without any doubt. Used to emphasize that a situation or fact is obvious to everyone. It can also be used sarcastically.

## Example
- She clearly didn't read the instructions.
- He's clearly not over her. Look at the way he acts.

## Related Expressions
- obviously
- plainly
- evidently


---

# genuinely

## Meaning
Truly; sincerely; really, but with an added sense of heartfelt honesty. Used when emphasizing real feelings, opinions, or situations.

## Example
- I genuinely have no idea what to do right now.
- She genuinely cares about the people around her.

## Related Expressions
- sincerely
- truly
- honestly


---

# occasionally

## Meaning
Sometimes; now and then; not often, but happening from time to time. More polished than "sometimes."

## Example
- I occasionally have a coffee in the afternoon, but not every day.
- We occasionally argue, but we always work it out.

## Related Expressions
- sometimes
- every now and then
- from time to time


---

# randomly

## Meaning
Suddenly and unexpectedly; without a predictable pattern. Often used when something happens without warning or reason.

## Example
- I randomly ran into my old teacher at the grocery store.
- Random, but I've been thinking about you lately.

## Related Expressions
- out of the blue
- unexpectedly
- suddenly


---

# typically

## Meaning
Usually; generally; most of the time. Used when talking about common habits or patterns, but allows for exceptions.

## Example
- I typically wake up around 7, but today I slept in.
- She typically finishes work early on Fridays.

## Related Expressions
- usually
- generally
- normally


---

# conventionally

## Meaning
According to accepted standards or customs; in the usual way. Used to describe what is typical in society or tradition.

## Example
- Conventionally, this role goes to someone with more experience.
- She doesn't dress conventionally, but it works for her.

## Related Expressions
- traditionally
- customarily
- usually
