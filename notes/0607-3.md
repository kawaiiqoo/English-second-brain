# I can't get enough of ~

## Meaning

This expression means you like something so much that you always want more of it. You never feel tired or bored of it.

## Example

I can't get enough of this chocolate cake. It's so delicious!

## Related Expressions

- I'm crazy about ~
- I'm hooked on ~
- I can't stop ~
