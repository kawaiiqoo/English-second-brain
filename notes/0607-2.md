# clearly

## Meaning
Used to emphasize that something is obvious, unmistakable, or easy to understand. It can highlight a situation where the answer or reason is evident. Sometimes, it is used with a slightly sarcastic or ironic tone.

## Example
- She clearly didn't read the instructions.
- He's clearly not over her. Look at the way he acts.

## Related Expressions
- obviously
- plainly
- evidently
- unmistakably

---

# genuinely

## Meaning
Truly; sincerely; really. Used to stress that something is honest, heartfelt, or authentic, often expressing true feelings or opinions in a stronger way than "really."

## Example
- I genuinely have no idea what to do right now.
- She genuinely cares about the people around her.

## Related Expressions
- sincerely
- honestly
- truly
- wholeheartedly

# occasionally

## Meaning
Sometimes, but not very often; from time to time. It sounds more sophisticated than "sometimes" and means that something happens irregularly, but not frequently.

## Example
- I occasionally have a coffee in the afternoon but not every day.
- We occasionally argue but we always work it out.

## Related Expressions
- sometimes
- from time to time
- every so often
- once in a while

---

# randomly

## Meaning
Unexpectedly; without planning; out of the blue. Used to describe something that happens with no clear reason or a thought that appears suddenly. Also for smooth conversation transitions.

## Example
- I randomly ran into my old teacher at the grocery store.
- Random, but I've been thinking about you lately.

## Related Expressions
- out of the blue
- suddenly
- unexpectedly
- by chance

---

# typically

## Meaning
Usually; in most cases; normally. Used to describe a common pattern or habit, while leaving room for exceptions.

## Example
- I typically wake up around 7 but today I slept in.
- She typically finishes work early on Fridays.

## Related Expressions
- usually
- normally
- generally
- as a rule

---

# conventionally

## Meaning
According to tradition, customs, or usual standards. Used when something follows commonly accepted rules or behaviors.

## Example
- Conventionally, this role goes to someone with more experience.
- She doesn't dress conventionally but it works for her.

## Related Expressions
- traditionally
- customarily
- ordinarily
- according to convention
