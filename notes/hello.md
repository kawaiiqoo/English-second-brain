# I couldn't agree more

## Meaning

This expression is used to show that you completely agree with what someone has just said. It emphasizes strong agreement.

## Example

A: "I think learning English will help us in our future careers."
B: "I couldn't agree more!"

## Related Expressions

- I agree completely.
- Absolutely!
- You’re absolutely right.
- I totally agree.
