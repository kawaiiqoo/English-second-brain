# Clearly

## Meaning  
Used to emphasize that something is obvious or easy to see/understand. Can also have a slightly sarcastic tone.

## Example  
- She clearly didn't read the instructions.  
  (걔 분명히 설명서를 안 읽었어.)
- He's clearly not over her. Look at the way he acts.  
  (걔 분명히 아직 못 잊은 거야. 행동을 봐봐.)

## Related Expressions  
- obviously  
- evidently  
- plainly

# Genuinely

## Meaning  
Sincerely; truly. Used to express real feelings or opinions with extra weight, more heartfelt than "really."

## Example  
- I genuinely have no idea what to do right now.  
  (진심으로 지금 뭘 해야 할지 모르겠어.)
- She genuinely cares about the people around her.  
  (걔는 주변 사람들을 진심으로 아껴.)

## Related Expressions  
- truly
- sincerely
- honestly

# Occasionally

## Meaning  
Sometimes; from time to time. More refined than "sometimes," used for things that don’t happen often but do occur.

## Example  
- I occasionally have a coffee in the afternoon but not every day.  
  (오후에 가끔 커피 마시는데 매일은 아니야.)
- We occasionally argue but we always work it out.  
  (우리가끔 싸우는데 항상 잘 해결해.)

## Related Expressions  
- sometimes  
- from time to time  
- every so often

# Randomly

## Meaning  
Suddenly or without any pattern; unexpectedly. Used for things that happen by chance, or to switch topics in conversation.

## Example  
- I randomly ran into my old teacher at the grocery store.  
  (마트에서 뜬금없이 옛날 선생님을 만났어.)
- Random, but I've been thinking about you lately.  
  (뜬금없는데 요즘 네 생각이 자꾸 났어.)

## Related Expressions  
- out of the blue  
- unexpectedly  
- by chance

# Typically

## Meaning  
Usually; in most cases, but not always. Implies a common pattern with exceptions possible.

## Example  
- I typically wake up around 7 but today I slept in.  
  (보통은 7시쯤 일어나는데 오늘은 늦잠 잤어.)
- She typically finishes work early on Fridays.  
  (걔보통 금요일엔 일찍 끝내.)

## Related Expressions  
- usually  
- normally  
- generally  


---

# Conventionally

## Meaning  
According to general agreement, custom, or common practice. Used to describe things that follow traditional or usual methods.

## Example  
- Conventionally, this role goes to someone with more experience.  
  (관례적으로 이 역할은 경험이 더 많은 사람한테 가.)
- She doesn't dress conventionally but it works for her.  
  (걔 통상적인 방식으로 옷을 입지 않는데 걔한테는 잘 어울려.)

## Related Expressions  
- traditionally  
- customarily  
- by the book
