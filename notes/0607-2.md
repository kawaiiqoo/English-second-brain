# clearly

## Meaning
Used to emphasize that something is obvious or easy to see/understand. It can also be used sarcastically to point out something that should have been noticed.  
(분명히, 누구나 알 수 있게 명확한 상황을 강조할 때. 비꼬는 느낌으로도 사용됨.)

## Example
She clearly didn’t read the instructions.  
(걔 분명히 설명서를 안 읽었어.)

## Related Expressions
- obviously
- plainly
- evidently

# genuinely

## Meaning
Truly, sincerely, or honestly. Used to express heartfelt emotion or opinion, much stronger than "really."  
(진심으로, 정말로. really보다 강하고 진정성 있는 강조.)

## Example
I genuinely have no idea what to do right now.  
(진심으로 지금 뭘 해야 할지 모르겠어.)

## Related Expressions
- sincerely
- honestly
- truly

# occasionally

## Meaning
Sometimes; less often than "often", more elegant than "sometimes." Describes things that happen now and then.  
(가끔, 이따금씩. sometimes보다 세련되고 빈도가 약간 낮은 느낌.)

## Example
We occasionally argue but we always work it out.  
(우리 가끔 싸우는데 항상 잘 해결해.)

## Related Expressions
- sometimes
- every so often
- from time to time

---

# randomly

## Meaning
Suddenly or without any plan or pattern. Used for unexpected events or to switch topics out of the blue.  
(갑자기, 뜬금없이. 예상치 못한 상황이나 대화 전환 시 사용.)

## Example
I randomly ran into my old teacher at the grocery store.  
(마트에서 뜬금없이 옛날 선생님을 만났어.)

## Related Expressions
- out of the blue
- unexpectedly
- by chance

---

# typically

## Meaning
Usually or generally; describes something that is a common pattern but allows for exceptions.  
(보통은, 일반적으로, 대개. 예외가 있음을 암시.)

## Example
She typically finishes work early on Fridays.  
(걔 보통 금요일엔 일찍 끝내.)

## Related Expressions
- usually
- generally
- as a rule

---

# conventionally

## Meaning
According to usual or traditional ways; according to social norms or standards.  
(관례적으로, 통상적으로. 사회적 기준이나 일반적으로 받아들여지는 방식을 표현.)

## Example
Conventionally, this role goes to someone with more experience.  
(관례적으로 이 역할은 경험이 더 많은 사람한테 가.)

## Related Expressions
- traditionally
- customarily
- ordinarily
