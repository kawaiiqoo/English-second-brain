# clearly

## Meaning
- Obviously; without any doubt.  
- Used to emphasize that something is easy to see, understand, or realize—even sometimes in a sarcastic way.

## Example
- She clearly didn't read the instructions.  
- He's clearly not over her. Look at the way he acts.

## Related Expressions
- obviously
- evidently
- plainly


---

# genuinely

## Meaning
- Truly; sincerely; in a real and honest way.  
- Stronger than "really," expressing heartfelt feeling or true intention.

## Example
- I genuinely have no idea what to do right now.
- She genuinely cares about the people around her.

## Related Expressions
- truly
- sincerely
- honestly


---

# occasionally

## Meaning
- Sometimes, but not often.  
- A more refined or formal way to say "sometimes."

## Example
- I occasionally have a coffee in the afternoon, but not every day.
- We occasionally argue but we always work it out.

## Related Expressions
- sometimes
- from time to time
- once in a while


---

# randomly

## Meaning
- Without a specific pattern, order, or reason.  
- Used for unexpected events or thoughts; helps shift topics casually.

## Example
- I randomly ran into my old teacher at the grocery store.
- Random, but I've been thinking about you lately.

## Related Expressions
- suddenly
- out of the blue
- unexpectedly


---

# typically

## Meaning
- Usually; generally; in most cases.  
- Indicates a general pattern with room for exceptions.

## Example
- I typically wake up around 7, but today I slept in.
- She typically finishes work early on Fridays.

## Related Expressions
- usually
- generally
- normally


---

# conventionally

## Meaning
- According to accepted customs or standards; traditionally.  
- Used to describe commonly accepted habits or standards.

## Example
- Conventionally, this role goes to someone with more experience.
- She doesn't dress conventionally but it works for her.

## Related Expressions
- traditionally
- customarily
- typically
