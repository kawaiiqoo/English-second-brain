# clearly

## Meaning
Used to emphasize that something is obvious to everyone; it can also have a slightly sarcastic nuance.

## Example
- She clearly didn't read the instructions.
- He's clearly not over her. Look at the way he acts.

## Related Expressions
- obviously
- evidently
- plainly

---

# genuinely

## Meaning
Truly or sincerely; much stronger and more heartfelt than "really" when expressing feelings or opinions.

## Example
- I genuinely have no idea what to do right now.
- She genuinely cares about the people around her.

## Related Expressions
- truly
- sincerely
- honestly

---

# occasionally

## Meaning
Sometimes; a more polished way to say something happens from time to time, but not often.

## Example
- I occasionally have a coffee in the afternoon but not every day.
- We occasionally argue but we always work it out.

## Related Expressions
- sometimes
- from time to time
- every so often

---

# randomly

## Meaning
Suddenly or without plan; used when something unexpected happens or when a topic changes unexpectedly in conversation.

## Example
- I randomly ran into my old teacher at the grocery store.
- Random, but I've been thinking about you lately.

## Related Expressions
- out of the blue
- unexpectedly
- all of a sudden

---

# typically

## Meaning
Usually or normally; describes a general pattern or habit, allowing for some exceptions.

## Example
- I typically wake up around 7 but today I slept in.
- She typically finishes work early on Fridays.

## Related Expressions
- usually
- normally
- generally

---

# conventionally

## Meaning
According to common or accepted customs, standards, or traditions.

## Example
- Conventionally, this role goes to someone with more experience.
- She doesn't dress conventionally but it works for her.

## Related Expressions
- traditionally
- customarily
- in general
