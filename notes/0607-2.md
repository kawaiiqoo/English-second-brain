# clearly

## Meaning
Definitely, obviously, without doubt. Used to emphasize that something is obvious or undeniable. Sometimes used sarcastically.

## Example
- She clearly didn't read the instructions.  
  (걔 분명히 설명서를 안 읽었어.)

## Related Expressions
- obviously
- plainly
- evidently

---

# genuinely

## Meaning
Sincerely, truly, with real honesty or emotion. Shows deep sincerity, more heartfelt than "really."

## Example
- I genuinely have no idea what to do right now.  
  (진심으로 지금 뭘 해야 할지 모르겠어.)

## Related Expressions
- sincerely
- truly
- honestly

---

# occasionally

## Meaning
Sometimes, now and then, not regularly but from time to time. More refined than "sometimes."

## Example
- I occasionally have a coffee in the afternoon but not every day.  
  (오후에 가끔 커피 마시는데 매일은 아니야.)

## Related Expressions
- sometimes
- every so often
- from time to time

---

# randomly

## Meaning
Unexpectedly, without planning or pattern. Used when something happens by chance or out of the blue.

## Example
- I randomly ran into my old teacher at the grocery store.  
  (마트에서 뜬금없이 옛날 선생님을 만났어.)

## Related Expressions
- out of the blue
- by chance
- unexpectedly

---

# typically

## Meaning
Normally, usually, in most cases. Describes common patterns or usual behavior, but allows for exceptions.

## Example
- I typically wake up around 7 but today I slept in.  
  (보통은 7시쯤 일어나는데 오늘은 늦잠 잤어.)

## Related Expressions
- usually
- generally
- ordinarily

---

# conventionally

## Meaning
According to tradition, custom, or standard practice. Used when talking about what is generally accepted or done.

## Example
- Conventionally, this role goes to someone with more experience.  
  (관례적으로 이 역할은 경험이 더 많은 사람한테 가.)

## Related Expressions
- traditionally
- customarily
- normatively
