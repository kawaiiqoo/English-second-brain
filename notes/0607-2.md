# clearly

## Meaning
Used to emphasize that something is obvious, certain, or easy to see. Sometimes used with a hint of sarcasm.

## Example
- She clearly didn't read the instructions.  
  (걔 분명히 설명서를 안 읽었어.)
- He's clearly not over her. Look at the way he acts.  
  (걔 분명히 아직 못 잊은 거야. 행동을 봐봐.)

## Related Expressions
- obviously
- evidently
- for sure
- without a doubt

# genuinely

## Meaning
Used to emphasize sincerity or truthfulness. Stronger and more heartfelt than “really.”

## Example
- I genuinely have no idea what to do right now.  
  (진심으로 지금 뭘 해야 할지 모르겠어.)
- She genuinely cares about the people around her.  
  (걔는 주변 사람들을 진심으로 아껴.)

## Related Expressions
- truly
- honestly
- sincerely
- from the heart

# occasionally

## Meaning
Happening sometimes but not often. More refined than “sometimes.”

## Example
- I occasionally have a coffee in the afternoon, but not every day.  
  (오후에 가끔 커피 마시는데 매일은 아니야.)
- We occasionally argue but always work it out.  
  (우리가끔 싸우는데 항상 잘 해결해.)

## Related Expressions
- sometimes
- from time to time
- every so often
- once in a while

# randomly

## Meaning
Happening without a specific reason, order, or expectation. Used when something happens unexpectedly or to change the topic.

## Example
- I randomly ran into my old teacher at the grocery store.  
  (마트에서 뜬금없이 옛날 선생님을 만났어.)
- Random, but I’ve been thinking about you lately.  
  (뜬금없는데 요즘 네 생각이 자꾸 났어.)

## Related Expressions
- out of the blue
- suddenly
- unexpectedly
- for no reason

---

# typically

## Meaning
Describes what usually happens; shows the normal or most common situation, though exceptions can exist.

## Example
- I typically wake up around 7, but today I slept in.  
  (보통은 7시쯤 일어나는데 오늘은 늦잠 잤어.)
- She typically finishes work early on Fridays.  
  (걔 보통 금요일엔 일찍 끝내.)

## Related Expressions
- usually
- normally
- generally
- as a rule

---

# conventionally

## Meaning
Used to describe things done according to what is generally accepted or traditional.

## Example
- Conventionally, this role goes to someone with more experience.  
  (관례적으로 이 역할은 경험이 더 많은 사람한테 가.)
- She doesn't dress conventionally, but it works for her.  
  (걔 통상적인 방식으로 옷을 입지 않는데 걔한테는 잘 어울려.)

## Related Expressions
- traditionally
- customarily
- commonly
- according to tradition
