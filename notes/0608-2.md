# On the same page

## Meaning
To have the same understanding or agreement about something; to think alike.

## Example
Let’s make sure we’re on the same page before we start the project.

## Related Expressions
- See eye to eye
- Be in sync
- Be on board

---

# Fill someone in

## Meaning
To give someone the details or information they missed.

## Example
I was late to the meeting—can you fill me in on what happened?

## Related Expressions
- Bring someone up to speed
- Update someone

---

# Bail on

## Meaning
