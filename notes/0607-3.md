# I can’t get enough of~

## Meaning
This expression is used to say that you love or enjoy something so much that you always want more of it.

## Example
I can’t get enough of chocolate; I could eat it every day!

## Related Expressions
- I’m hooked on~
- I’m addicted to~
- I just love~
- I can’t stop~
