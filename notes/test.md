# I can't help ~ing

## Meaning
This expression is used when you want to say that you cannot stop yourself from doing something or you do something involuntarily. It is often used for feelings or thoughts that are hard to control.

## Example
- I can't help worrying about my future.  
  (내 미래가 걱정될 수밖에 없어.)
- I can't help thinking that we made the wrong decision.  
  (우리가 잘못된 결정을 내린 것 같다는 생각이 자꾸 들어.)

## Related Expressions
- I can’t stop ~ing  
  (예: I can't stop thinking about you.)
- I can't resist ~  
  (예: I can't resist chocolate.)
- I can't avoid ~ing  
  (예: I can't avoid feeling nervous before exams.)
