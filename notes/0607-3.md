# can't help ~ing

## Meaning

"Can't help ~ing" means you cannot stop yourself from doing something, even if you want to. It describes a feeling or action that is uncontrollable or unavoidable.

## Example

- I can't help worrying about my future.  
  (I worry about my future, even though I might not want to.)
- I can't help thinking that we made the wrong decision.  
  (I keep having the thought that we made the wrong decision.)
- I can't help laughing when I watch that video.  
  (I always laugh when I watch that video, even if I try not to.)

## Related Expressions

- can't stop ~ing  
  (Similar meaning, but "can't help ~ing" is used more for thoughts or feelings)
  - I can't stop thinking about her.
- can't resist  
  (Used for temptations or urges)
  - I can't resist eating chocolate.
- can't bring myself to ~  
  (Refers to being unable to do something because of emotions)
  - I can't bring myself to delete those photos.
