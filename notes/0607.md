# clearly

## Meaning
Used to emphasize that something is obvious or easy to understand. Can also be used with a slightly sarcastic tone.

## Example
- She clearly didn't read the instructions.
- He's clearly not over her. Look at the way he acts.

## Related Expressions
- obviously
- plainly
- evidently

---

# genuinely

## Meaning
Sincerely; truly. Used to stress honest feelings or opinions. Stronger and more heartfelt than "really."

## Example
- I genuinely have no idea what to do right now.
- She genuinely cares about the people around her.

## Related Expressions
- sincerely
- honestly
- truly

---

# occasionally

## Meaning
Sometimes; not often but from time to time. More refined than "sometimes" for describing things that happen now and then.

## Example
- I occasionally have a coffee in the afternoon but not every day.
- We occasionally argue but we always work it out.

## Related Expressions
- sometimes
- from time to time
- every so often

---

# randomly

## Meaning
Unexpectedly; without a plan or pattern. Used when something happens suddenly or with no clear reason.

## Example
- I randomly ran into my old teacher at the grocery store.
- Random but I've been thinking about you lately.

## Related Expressions
- out of the blue
- unexpectedly
- by chance

---

# typically

## Meaning
Usually; in most cases. Used to describe general habits or patterns but allows for exceptions.

## Example
- I typically wake up around 7 but today I slept in.
- She typically finishes work early on Fridays.

## Related Expressions
- usually
- generally
- normally

---

# conventionally

## Meaning
In a way that follows traditional or accepted standards.

## Example
- Conventionally, this role goes to someone with more experience.
- She doesn't dress conventionally but it works for her.

## Related Expressions
- traditionally
- customarily
- ordinarily
