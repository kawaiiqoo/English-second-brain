# I can’t help ~ing

## Meaning

"I can’t help ~ing" means you cannot stop yourself from feeling or doing something, even if you want to. It expresses that a feeling or action is impossible to avoid.

## Example

- I can’t help worrying about my future.  
  (내 미래가 걱정될 수밖에 없어.)
- I can’t help thinking that we made the wrong decision.  
  (우리가 잘못된 결정을 내린 것 같다는 생각이 자꾸 들어.)

## Related Expressions

- I can’t stop ~ing (멈출 수 없다)
- I can’t resist ~ing (참을 수 없다)
- I can’t avoid ~ing (피할 수 없다)
