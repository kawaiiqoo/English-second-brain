# Clearly

## Meaning
Used to emphasize that something is obvious or easy to see. It can also be used sarcastically to point out that something should have been obvious.

## Example
She clearly didn't read the instructions.  
(그녀는 분명히 설명서를 안 읽었어.)

## Related Expressions
- Obviously
- Plainly
- Evidently

---

# Genuinely

## Meaning
Honestly or truly; with real feeling or sincerity. Stronger and deeper than "really."

## Example
She genuinely cares about the people around her.  
(그녀는 주변 사람들을 진심으로 아껴.)

## Related Expressions
- Truly
- Really (less strong)
- Honestly

---

# Occasionally

## Meaning
Sometimes or from time to time, but not often. More refined than "sometimes."

## Example
I occasionally have a coffee in the afternoon, but not every day.  
(오후에 가끔 커피 마시는데 매일은 아니야.)

## Related Expressions
- Sometimes
- From time to time
- Now and then

---

# Randomly

## Meaning
Suddenly, without warning or pattern; unexpectedly. Can also be used to quickly change the topic in conversation.

## Example
I randomly ran into my old teacher at the grocery store.  
(마트에서 뜬금없이 옛날 선생님을 만났어.)

## Related Expressions
- Out of the blue
- Unexpectedly
- All of a sudden

---

# Typically

## Meaning
Usually; in most cases but allowing for exceptions. Describes normal routines or patterns.

## Example
She typically finishes work early on Fridays.  
(그녀는 보통 금요일엔 일찍 끝내.)

## Related Expressions
- Usually
- Normally
- Generally

---

# Conventionally

## Meaning
According to what is generally done or accepted; following social norms or tradition.

## Example
Conventionally, this role goes to someone with more experience.  
(관례적으로 이 역할은 경험이 더 많은 사람한테 가.)

## Related Expressions
- Traditionally
- Commonly
- Customarily
