# clearly

## Meaning
Used to emphasize that something is obvious or evident. Can also be used sarcastically to point out the obvious.

## Example
- She clearly didn't read the instructions.  
- He's clearly not over her. Look at the way he acts.

## Related Expressions
- obviously
- evidently
- plainly

---

# genuinely

## Meaning
Truly; sincerely; used to emphasize real feeling, honesty, or authentic opinion. Stronger and more heartfelt than "really."

## Example
- I genuinely have no idea what to do right now.
- She genuinely cares about the people around her.

## Related Expressions
- honestly
- sincerely
- truly

---

# occasionally

## Meaning
Sometimes, but not regularly; from time to time. More sophisticated than "sometimes"; describes things that happen infrequently.

## Example
- I occasionally have a coffee in the afternoon but not every day.
- We occasionally argue but we always work it out.

## Related Expressions
- from time to time
- once in a while
- every so often

---

# randomly

## Meaning
Suddenly or unexpectedly; without a planned reason or pattern. Also used when something happens by chance.

## Example
- I randomly ran into my old teacher at the grocery store.
- Random, but I've been thinking about you lately.

## Related Expressions
- out of the blue
- by chance
- unexpectedly

---

# typically

## Meaning
Usually or generally; describes what normally happens, but implies there are exceptions.

## Example
- I typically wake up around 7 but today I slept in.
- She typically finishes work early on Fridays.

## Related Expressions
- usually
- normally
- generally

---

# conventionally

## Meaning
According to what is traditionally or generally done; refers to accepted standards or customs.

## Example
- Conventionally, this role goes to someone with more experience.
- She doesn't dress conventionally but it works for her.

## Related Expressions
- traditionally
- customarily
- habitually
