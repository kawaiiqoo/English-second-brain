# clearly

## Meaning
Used to emphasize that something is obvious or easy to see. It can also have a slightly sarcastic tone.

## Example
- She clearly didn't read the instructions.  
- He's clearly not over her. Look at the way he acts.

## Related Expressions
- obviously
- plainly
- evidently

---

# genuinely

## Meaning
Used to show that you are sincere or honest, often stronger and more heartfelt than "really."

## Example
- I genuinely have no idea what to do right now.
- She genuinely cares about the people around her.

## Related Expressions
- sincerely
- truly
- honestly

---

# occasionally

## Meaning
Used to talk about something that happens from time to time, but not regularly. More sophisticated than "sometimes."

## Example
- I occasionally have a coffee in the afternoon but not every day.
- We occasionally argue but we always work it out.

## Related Expressions
- sometimes
- from time to time
- every once in a while

---

# randomly

## Meaning
Used to describe something happening suddenly or without a plan; often for unexpected or unplanned situations.

## Example
- I randomly ran into my old teacher at the grocery store.
- Randomly, I've been thinking about you lately.

## Related Expressions
- out of the blue
- unexpectedly
- by chance

---

# typically

## Meaning
Used to describe what usually happens or what is usual in a situation, but allows for exceptions.

## Example
- I typically wake up around 7 but today I slept in.
- She typically finishes work early on Fridays.

## Related Expressions
- usually
- generally
- normally

---

# conventionally

## Meaning
Describes something done according to traditional or accepted standards; what is normal or expected by habits or customs.

## Example
- Conventionally, this role goes to someone with more experience.
- She doesn't dress conventionally but it works for her.

## Related Expressions
- traditionally
- customarily
- ordinarily
